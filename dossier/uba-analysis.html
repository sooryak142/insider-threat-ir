Credential abuse vs. credential theft

A central question for attribution: was this the real user, or someone using his stolen account? The identity evidence points firmly to abuse by the legitimate owner.

SignalObservationReading
Source geolocationSingle consistent home ISP IP (203.0.113.45), same city as residence on fileNo impossible travel → not a remote attacker
MFAEvery session satisfied push MFA on the subject's enrolled device, approved in secondsDevice possession → the owner himself
Device & user agentSame corporate laptop and browser fingerprint as his normal workNo new/unknown device
Failed-login patternNo brute force, password spray or anomalous auth failures beforehandNo compromise precursor
BehaviourActions required insider knowledge of exactly where the valuable data livedFamiliarity consistent with the employee

Conclusion: credential abuse by the account owner, assessed high confidence. This matters for response — the fix is identity governance and offboarding, not a password reset.
