The two gaps, and the fixes

Gap 1 — encrypted uploads to new domains

DLP can't inspect encrypted content, so the decision has to move up to context: a large encrypted upload to a newly-registered, uncategorised domain is itself high-risk. Fix: treat large encrypted egress as high-risk, and block newly-registered/uncategorised domains for sensitive groups.

Gap 2 — USB in audit mode

Device control saw and logged the 4.3 GB write but was not set to block it for R&D. Fix: move R&D removable-media policy from audit to block; permit only approved, encrypted devices.

Both succeeding channels are mapped to T1567.002 (cloud) and T1052.001 (USB) on the ATT&CK page, and every network indicator is in the IOC matrix.
