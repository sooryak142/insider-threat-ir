Reading the mapping

Why no "malware" techniques

This is a living-off-the-land insider case. There is no phishing, no exploit and no implant — the actor used legitimate access and built-in or portable tools. That is typical of insider threats and is exactly why behavioural detection (UBA) mattered more than signature-based antivirus here.

Observed vs. attempted

Two Defense-Evasion techniques were attempted but failed: the DLP tamper attempt (tamper protection held) and — in effect — log clearing (the SIEM already had the logs). We map attempted techniques too, because the attempt is itself high-value detection signal.
